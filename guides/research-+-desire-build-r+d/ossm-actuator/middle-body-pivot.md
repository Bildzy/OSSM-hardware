### Step 1: Prep middle body

<mark style="color:red;">IMAGE PLACEHOLDER</mark>

1. **Add nuts to body**
   1. Place the middle body down on your work surface with the middle body nut slots facing up.
   2. Thread 1 middle body nut (M5) onto the end of 1 middle body bolt (M5x35mm).
   3. Grabbing the bolt by the socket head, place the middle body nut into one of the slots on the middle body, and push it down into place.
   4. When it has been pressed all the way in, unscrew/remove the bolt and repeat with the remaining middle body nuts.
2. **Place on work surface**
