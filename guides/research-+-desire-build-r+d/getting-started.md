#### **Tightness levels**

When referring to levels of tightness use the list below.

* **Finger-tight** – tighten with fingers until the bolt is seated, with no tool force yet.
* **Snug** - light tool pressure, just enough to hold parts in place without movement.
* **Firm** – moderate tool pressure so the fastener is secure but not over-tightened.
* **Fully Tight / Secure** – strong tool pressure to achieve final tightness.
